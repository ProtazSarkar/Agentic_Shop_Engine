## Core Features & Views

### 1. Catalog & Product Discovery
* **Home Page**: Featured collections, promotional banners, and trending product grids.
* **Shop / Product Listing**: Dynamic product grid with filtering sidebar (filter by category, price range, and sorting options).
* **Product Detail Page**: Detailed views showcasing descriptions, stock indicators, high-res images, and direct "Add to Cart" actions.

### 2. Shopping Cart & Checkout
* **Cart Slide-over / Page**: Real-time summary of selected items, quantity adjustment controls, item removal, and subtotal calculation.
* **Checkout Flow**: Secure multi-step checkout form capturing shipping address, order confirmation, and completion summary.

### 3. Authentication UI
* **Login / Signup Pages**: Clean email/password authentication forms with validation.
* **Social Login Integration**: "Continue with Google" OAuth trigger butt
